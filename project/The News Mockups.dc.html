try it.

  1. Scroll or swipe on the phone. Cards snap one per screen.
  2. The current segment fills over nine seconds. Nothing auto-advances; the chevron pulses once when time is up.
  3. Move on early and the segment snaps to full.
  4. Tap heart, share and bookmark. Tap "read full article →" for the reader sheet; tap outside it to close.
  5. The last card is the caught-up state.
